Lung Cancer.

Lung cancer is the world's leading cause of cancer death. Screening for lung 
cancer by low-dose computed tomography improves mortality. Various modalities 
exist for diagnosis and staging. Treatment is determined by subtype and stage of 
cancer; there are several personalized therapies that did not exist just a few 
years ago. Caring for the patient with lung cancer is a complex task. This 
review provides a broad outline of this disease, helping clinicians identify 
such patients and familiarizing them with lung cancer care options, so they are 
better equipped to guide their patients along this challenging journey.
